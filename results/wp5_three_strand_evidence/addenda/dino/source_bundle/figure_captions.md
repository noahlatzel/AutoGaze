## compute_fidelity

Final normalized CLS cosine distance to dense DINOv3 ViT-S/16 on 12 frozen validation videos (two per each of six sources; 16 full-field 224 × 224 frames/video). Recorded per-video means are averaged within sources, then sources equally. Lower distance means greater representation fidelity, not higher downstream accuracy. Left: analytical encoder matrix-multiply GFLOPs/frame including dense patch embedding, FMA=2; matching dot products are excluded from this axis and reported separately in tables. ToMe progressively merges after attention; r=5/10/15 finish at 136/76/16 patches. Temporary spatial r=15 merges before each block then restores 196 patches. K16 retains 16 patches throughout all blocks. Error bars show descriptive video-bootstrap 90% intervals. Right: individual macro means of six human training endpoints and 16 random-mask replicates; diamonds denote family means. Their spread is distinct from video-bootstrap uncertainty. Post-hoc descriptive 90% paired source-stratified video bootstrap interval; 10,000 draws, seed 20260909; conditional on the six fixed sources, six observed training endpoints and 16 observed random-mask replicates. Frames, seeds and timing repeats are not resampled.

## k16_paired_differences

Paired equal-source mean CLS-distance differences: the mean of six human-trained K16 endpoints minus each K16 control, on identical videos and frames. The random control is the mean of 16 fixed random-mask replicates. Negative values favor the human mean. Whiskers are 90% descriptive intervals; these are unadjusted exploratory comparisons, not confirmatory superiority tests. Post-hoc descriptive 90% paired source-stratified video bootstrap interval; 10,000 draws, seed 20260909; conditional on the six fixed sources, six observed training endpoints and 16 observed random-mask replicates. Frames, seeds and timing repeats are not resampled.

## latency_protocols

Measured cached-mask encoder forward latency on NVIDIA RTX A2000 12GB, float32, with three warmups and ten timing repeats. Left: batch-16 input calls, displayed as amortized milliseconds per frame; video medians are averaged equally within each source and then across sources. Whiskers show the same descriptive paired source-stratified video bootstrap. The retained K16 implementation processes per-image ragged sequences internally. Spatial batch-16 timing was not measured. Right: separate batch-one experiment on the two predeclared videos, showing both video medians and their mean; no population confidence interval. Each repeat sums 16 individually synchronized frame forwards before division by 16. Human markers average the six endpoint medians; the random marker uses only declared seed 2026090900. Both protocols time current implementations on normalized device-resident frames and cached masks, including mask application/merging and wrapper bookkeeping. Selector inference, input preparation, data transfer and downstream heads are excluded. Arm order was fixed; small timing differences do not establish policy speed rankings. Timing repetition and video/seed variability remain separate.
